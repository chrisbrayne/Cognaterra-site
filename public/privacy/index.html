Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 • Cognaterra Limited (Company No. 16858068)

1. Introduction & Overview

Cognaterra Limited (“Cognaterra”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is a private limited company registered in England and Wales under Company Registration Number 16858068. We provide strategic consulting, AI horizon scanning, and digital transformation advisory services for the cultural heritage and archaeological sectors.

This Privacy Policy sets out how we collect, process, store, and protect personal data when you visit our website (https://cognaterra.co.uk), engage our advisory services, or interact with our integrated applications and software tools (including integrations connecting to third-party services such as Google Workspace).

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information solely to the extent necessary to deliver our advisory, consulting, and technological workflows:

  • Direct Communications: Information provided when contacting us via email, enquiry forms, or meetings (e.g. name, professional email address, organisation name, job title, and message content).
  • Client Engagement Data: Strategic, operational, or technical documentation provided by client organisations during advisory, evaluation, or digital governance engagements.
  • Google User Data (OAuth): When you explicitly authorise our applications or developer tools to access your Google account via OAuth 2.0 (for example, Google Workspace APIs including Google Calendar, Gmail, Google Drive, or Google Docs), our application accesses only the specific scopes requested and granted by you (such as profile details, calendar event scheduling, or communication drafts).
  • Technical & Log Data: Standard server logs, IP addresses, browser types, and operating system data collected automatically for website diagnostics, security auditing, and performance monitoring.

3. Purpose & Legal Basis for Processing

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and Data Protection Act 2018, we process personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a Contract: To deliver requested digital transformation, strategy, horizon scanning, and software tooling services.
  • Legitimate Interests: To respond to professional enquiries, maintain infrastructure security, prevent fraudulent activity, and ensure service reliability.
  • User Consent: Where you explicitly authorise third-party integrations (e.g. Google OAuth consent) to connect your accounts and execute automated tasks.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with statutory UK corporate, accounting, and taxation record-keeping obligations.

4. Google API Services & User Data Policy Compliance

Cognaterra applications, tools, and integrations strictly adhere to Google’s security, data handling, and developer requirements.

Cognaterra’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • No Sale of Google User Data: We never sell, lease, commercialise, or trade Google user data to any third party under any circumstances.
  • No Advertising: We never use or transfer Google user data for serving advertisements, retargeting, behavioural targeting, or interest-based advertising.
  • No AI/ML Model Training: Google user data accessed via Google Workspace or Google APIs is never used to train, retrain, or fine-tune generalised machine learning or artificial intelligence models.
  • Restricted Human Access: Human personnel do not read or view your Google user data unless you have given explicit prior consent for debugging, where strictly required for security investigations, or where mandated by law.

5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, or disclose personal data to third parties for commercial or promotional purposes. Information is shared only in the following controlled circumstances:

  • Essential Infrastructure Providers: Vetted cloud infrastructure, hosting (e.g. GitHub Pages), and authenticated software providers operating under robust data processing agreements compliant with UK GDPR.
  • Statutory & Legal Authorities: If required by law, court order, or governmental authorities (e.g. HM Revenue and Customs or the Information Commissioner’s Office).

6. Data Storage, Security & Retention

We apply technical and organisational measures to prevent unauthorised access, modification, disclosure, or loss of information:

  • All web communications are encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS / HTTPS encryption.
  • OAuth tokens and credentials are encrypted and stored in secure, access-controlled environments.
  • Data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the operational purpose for which it was collected, or to comply with statutory UK limitation periods and legal obligations.

7. User Controls, Access Revocation & Data Deletion

You maintain full control over your data and any third-party services connected to Cognaterra:

  • Revoking Google Access: You can revoke Cognaterra’s access to your Google account at any time via the official Google Security permissions dashboard at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Requesting Data Deletion: You may request the permanent deletion of your personal information, communication records, or cached data at any time by emailing contact@cognaterra.co.uk. We process all deletion requests promptly and confirm completion in writing.
  • Your Statutory Rights: Under UK GDPR, you have the right to request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).
